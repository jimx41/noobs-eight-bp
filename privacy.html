Noob's 8 Ball Pool

Privacy policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

Z Sphere ("we", "us") built the Noob's 8 Ball Pool app as a free game. This page explains how the app handles information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Noob's 8 Ball Pool does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile of any kind.

Network access

The app contains no networking code. It makes no web requests, contacts no servers of ours or anyone else's, and does not require the INTERNET permission to run. Everything it needs is packaged inside the app itself.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. No data is sold or shared, because none is gathered.

Device permissions

Noob's 8 Ball Pool requests no permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Your game exists only in memory while the app is open and is discarded when you close it. Nothing is written to storage, and nothing leaves your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information whatsoever, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children.

App stores

If you installed Noob's 8 Ball Pool from an app store, that store may collect information about the download under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that information, and it is governed by the store's terms rather than ours.

Changes

If a future version of the app adds any feature that handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
